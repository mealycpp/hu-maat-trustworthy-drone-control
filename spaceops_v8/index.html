05 / SWARMCHAT · AIRCRAFT DIAGNOSTIC ROOM

EVIDENCE-GROUNDED · NO DIRECT FLIGHT

Human ↔ five aircraft ↔ MA'AT. Chat with a drone or the whole group: say hello, ask how they are, who is closest to whom, who is highest, recent trends, or why something failed. These are deterministic logical actors: fixed rules and templates reading Gazebo telemetry, the health history and HU logs, not trained language models and not autonomous decision-makers. Each drone has a fixed persona (Horus watchful lead, Ra bright, Thoth analytical, Anubis careful guardian, Osiris calm) that changes only the wording; every number comes from telemetry and is UNKNOWN when the link is stale. Messages never dispatch motion commands.

06 / FAULT REJECTION & RECOVERY EXPERIMENT

SIMULATOR-ONLY · OPERATOR CONTROL

This experiment tests a simulated command-link fault, not real motor failure or real depleted battery. We inject it only in stable HOVER with sufficient separation, verify that Gazebo refuses further commands to the faulted drone, then explicitly clear the fault and observe recovery to hover. Automatic fleet re-formation is not yet validated.

01 STABLE HOVER02 INJECT SIM LINK FAULT03 VERIFY REFUSAL04 MANUAL CLEAR05 VERIFY TELEMETRY

All interactions are logged with UTC timestamps. This panel never lowers HU confidence thresholds or issues an autonomous formation transition.

Select a drone in stable HOVER. The operator must approve fault injection.
Use only in Gazebo. Control requests are bound to localhost, operator-authorized, and blocked if the aircraft is not in the required state.
