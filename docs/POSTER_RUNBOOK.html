2. 60-second pitch

"Speaking to robots is the most direct telepresence — but with drones, a misunderstanding has physics. HU decides before anything moves: execute, ask a human to confirm, or refuse. A 135M-parameter model on a laptop; it handles names it never saw. On our physical three-drone fleet: zero unsafe actions, all 13 invalid commands refused. Emergency words — stop, land now, khalas — bypass the AI entirely. MA'AT, the safety supervisor, checks every move. Here the same frozen model runs five drones in Gazebo — they now choose their own formations in team strategy rounds. Try it: give them a command."

4. Numbers to quote (all measured)

  • Physical fleet (paper): 60-command live protocol, 100% auto-execution precision, 0 unsafe accepts, 13/13 invalid commands refused.
  • 103 formations validated in live flight (letters, digits, constellations, Egyptian, sports, 3-D profiles).
  • Coordination pays: team-chosen moves formed ~10% faster than random (4.04 s vs 4.47 s, 12 flights each — early result).

5. Honest answers

  • Real or simulated? Paper results: physical CoDrones. Here: same frozen model + safety pipeline in Gazebo.
  • Is that an LLM talking? No — decisions are computed from real positions; sentences are templates; badges say so.
  • What if it's wrong? Uncertain → confirm, unsafe → refuse, emergencies skip the AI, MA'AT checks every move.
  • Future? More autonomy for the drones, always inside boundaries enforced by MA'AT.

6. If something goes wrong

Drones misbehave: type/say stop (hold) or land now in the HU console. Always works.
  • Page won't load → 4 · STATUS CHECK; if anything DOWN → 1 · START EVERYTHING.
  • Laptop slept → 1 · START EVERYTHING (2–3 min).
  • Show stuck → 2 · VISITOR then 3 · SHOW (it re-pads drones automatically if they landed bunched).
  • No sound → Windows volume; in SpaceOps click 🔊 again.
  • Keep the charger in and the lid open.
